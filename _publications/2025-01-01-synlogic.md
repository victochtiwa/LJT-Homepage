SynLogic introduces a novel approach for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale. The method is designed to improve logical reasoning capabilities in large language models (LLMs) and can be extended to other domains. The framework generates high-quality reasoning traces that can be verified, making them valuable for training models to reason more effectively.
